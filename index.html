SquadWatch is a free desktop app for macOS and Windows that plays up to four YouTube live streams side by side — so when your favorite streamers play together, you see every point of view.

What SquadWatch does with your Google account

Signing in is optional — you can always paste stream links instead. If you sign in, SquadWatch asks Google for read-only access to your YouTube account (youtube.readonly) and your basic profile, for one purpose: showing you which channels you subscribe to and which of them are live.

SquadWatch uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Read the SquadWatch Privacy Policy for the details.

  • Reads your subscriptions and public info about those channels and their videos.
  • Stays on your computer. There are no SquadWatch servers; nothing is sent to the developer.
  • Never posts, likes, comments, subscribes or changes anything on YouTube.
  • Never sells, shares or uses your data for ads.
  • Easy to undo: sign out or "Delete my data" in the app, or remove access in your Google account settings.
